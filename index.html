SoundCloud links play through the official SoundCloud player. Public tracks, playlists, and SoundCloud share links are supported. Some tracks have regional, sign-in, or embedding restrictions.

For real audio-reactive visuals: in desktop Chrome or Edge, select “Connect tab audio”, choose this browser tab, and enable “Share tab audio”. Only the audio is analyzed locally; no screen frames are used, recorded, or uploaded. Stop sharing at any time.

Local audio files connect directly to the analyzer. Open or drop MP3, WAV, OGG, or other audio your browser supports. The audio demo also runs through the same live analyzer.

SoundCloud’s iframe cannot expose raw audio to this page. Until tab audio is connected, SoundCloud uses an explicitly labeled ambient animation, not a simulated spectrum. Tab audio capture requires HTTPS or localhost and a supporting desktop browser.
